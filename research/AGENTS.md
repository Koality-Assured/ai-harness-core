# Research AGENTS

Per-topic deep dives under `research/<topic>/` — investigation notes and working inquiry records, not final org standards.

Ingest simply; do not duplicate skills or paste root Critical — link [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md). Promote durable standards to `docs/` and completed research packages to `results/research/`.

## Rules

- **Investigation Landing Zone:** When material research or technical deep dives are conducted, investigative findings and working inquiry notes MUST land under `research/<topic>/`.
- **Finished Research Packages:** When research culminates in a formal deliverable or publication-ready dossier for human handoff, route the finished artifact to `results/research/<topic>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/` per [`../results/results-conventions.md`](../results/results-conventions.md).
- Keep topic-scoped; link related `projects/` and `results/`.
- Specific Markdown filenames; topic `README.md` as human index only.
- Nested `AGENTS.md` only for unusually large multi-phase topics.
- If a catalogued skill owns material remaining work, spawn that `owner_agent`. Parent runs isolate CLI; session-end gates stay with the parent. Nested files MUST NOT undo root spawn-if-material.
