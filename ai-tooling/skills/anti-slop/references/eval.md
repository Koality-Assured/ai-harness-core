# Anti-slop eval checklist

Use after an edit. Pass/fail each; fix fails before returning. For detect mode, confirm each finding has a named pattern, quoted line, and short fix — no rewrite, score, or AI-authorship claim.

## Claims and voice

1. Meaning preserved; no invented facts, stats, quotes, or sources.
2. Distinctive voice kept; strong human lines left alone.
3. Cuts proportional to slop (not aggressive compression that erases character).

## Writing patterns

1. Banned filler and empty openers gone (unless quoted as examples).
2. Binary contrasts, colon drama, faux-insight, throat-clearing fixed.
3. Superficial *-ing* clauses, importance puffery, weasel attribution fixed.
4. Synonym cycling, forced threes, parataxis stacks, robotic rhythm fixed.
5. Fake-profound kickers and summary-recap endings removed.
6. Em dashes sparse; emoji headings / decorative mid-sentence bold removed.
7. Chatbot leftovers removed.

## Design / UI (when present)

1. No Inter+purple / generic cream-sage theme swap as the “fix.”
2. No centered eyebrow badge + three identical cards + 1-2-3 strip as default.
3. Empty/error copy is product-specific and actionable.
4. Layout shows point of view and structural variety.

## Final

1. Would a sharp colleague hear a person, not a template?
2. Edit mode: full draft + short What changed. Detect mode: findings only.
