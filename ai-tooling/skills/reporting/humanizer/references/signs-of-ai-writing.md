# Signs of AI writing (humanizer reference)

Condensed from [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) via [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) (MIT). Advisory — do not paste wholesale into deliverables. Keep claims; do not invent facts.

## Content

- Inflated importance/legacy (“pivotal moment”, “vital role”, “testament”)
- Name-dropping outlets/follower counts without useful context
- Shallow *-ing* analysis (“highlighting…”, “symbolizing…”)
- Sales language (nestled, vibrant, breathtaking, must-visit)
- Vague sources (“experts say”, “industry reports”)
- Formulaic challenges / future-outlook sections with no facts

## Language

- Overused AI words: delve, tapestry, pivotal, underscore, landscape (figurative), showcase, intricate, foster, enhance (as filler), etc.
- Avoiding *is*/*are* (“serves as”, “boasts”, “stands as”)
- Not X but Y / clipped negative endings
- Forced groups of three
- Synonym cycling; repeated sentence openings with no purpose
- False “from X to Y” ranges
- Passive voice that hides the actor

## Style

- Em/en dash addiction (unless writer sample uses them)
- Decorative bold; bold-label bullet lists
- Title Case In Every Heading
- Emoji as decoration
- Curly quotes when the target format uses straight quotes

## Chatbot leftovers

- “I hope this helps”, “Certainly!”, “Great question!”, “Would you like…”
- Knowledge-cutoff disclaimers and speculative gap-fills presented as fact
- Overly agreeable praise before the point

## Filler and rhetoric

- “In order to”, “due to the fact that”, stacked qualifiers
- Generic positive endings; fake deeper-truth phrases (“at its core”)
- Announcing the next point (“Let’s dive in”)
- Heading restated in the first sentence
- Forced punchlines / dramatic fragment stacks
- Formulaic sayings (“X is the Y of Z”)
- Fake-candid openers; answering objections nobody raised; rejecting fake alternatives

## Keep

- Every claim, name, number, quote, citation from the source
- Writer sample habits when provided
- Specific details, mixed feelings, genuine asides, varied sentence length

## False positives (do not over-flag)

Polish alone; one *however*; one em dash; curly quotes alone; useful legal/scope disclaimers; deliberate rhetorical repetition; quoted examples under discussion.
