# Banned / high-risk words and phrases

Improved extract from [anti-ai-slop-writing banned-words](https://github.com/jalaalrd/anti-ai-slop-writing) and [no-ai-slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop). Not a hard compiler — cut when used as filler; keep when quoted as examples or required technical terms (e.g. engineering “robust”).

## Vocabulary

delve, tapestry, realm (figurative), landscape (figurative), testament, vibrant, pivotal, intricate, meticulous, multifaceted, foster, leverage (verb hype), utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, cutting-edge, paradigm, transformative, groundbreaking, game-changer, seamless (hype), harness, embark, elevate, supercharge, underscore/highlight (as puffery), showcasing, navigating (figurative), spearheading, unprecedented, profound (hype), synergy, pain points, value-add, thought leadership

## Phrases / openers

- In today’s…
- It’s worth noting / important to note
- Let’s dive in / delve into
- At its core / in the realm of / when it comes to
- Here’s the thing / the deal
- Not just X, but Y / it’s not about X — it’s about Y
- At the end of the day / the bottom line is
- In conclusion / Overall, (as a closer)
- Firstly… Secondly… Thirdly…
- Unlock the power / take it to the next level / bridge the gap / move the needle
- I hope this helps / Certainly! / Absolutely! / Great question!
- As an AI… / As a language model…
- Moreover, / Furthermore, / Additionally, (stacked)

## Often-empty adverbs

just, literally, honestly, simply, actually, truly, fundamentally, importantly, crucially — cut when they add nothing; keep when they carry real contrast or voice.
